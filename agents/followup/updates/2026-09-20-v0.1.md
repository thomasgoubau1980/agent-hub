After a sales call, start a thread in #follow-up_channel and the agent does the reading: the recording, the email thread with the attendees, the HubSpot deal. It asks the few questions only a person can answer, keeps the brief in a canvas, and on the word **go** hands you a prompt for Dante in Distribute plus an email draft waiting in Superhuman. Nothing is sent or published by the agent.

- **One thread per call.** Everything about a follow-up lives in one Slack thread and its canvas, so Thomas can start it on the phone and Douceline can finish it at her desk.
- **Questions, not guesses.** Prices, references, hosting answers and dates come from you. Anything nobody confirms stays a visible placeholder on the page rather than an invented sentence.
- **Dante builds the page from the real transcript.** The agent writes the prompt, you paste it into Distribute, the page appears as a private draft in the Prospects folder.
- **The email is a draft, always.** It sits in Thomas's Superhuman as a reply on the prospect's own thread, with the page link added once the page exists.
- **Spellings from the CRM.** Company and people names come from HubSpot and email signatures, never from what the transcript thought it heard.
- **Voice notes work.** Answer in the thread by voice; the agent transcribes and quotes what it used.

Known limits in this version: the agent cannot create the page inside the Distribute app itself, so step 5 in the guide is a human paste; calls that live only in Granola or Fathom need Distribute's connection to those tools to be switched on before Dante can read them; HubSpot is read only, proposals for notes and tasks are posted in the thread for a tick.
