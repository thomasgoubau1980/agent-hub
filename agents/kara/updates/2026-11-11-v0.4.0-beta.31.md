title: The format bar on the iPhone Duo, and the Pencil stays on its day
---

- **The format bar shows on the iPhone.** iOS's own ⌃ ⌄ ✓ bar for form fields covered Kara's format bar above the keyboard, on the iPhone Duo for one. It is gone; the format bar now sits right on the keyboard.
- **The Pencil no longer changes the day.** Writing a word sideways with the Pencil, or Scribble, could flip the Day page to another day. Now only a finger swipe on the page itself changes the day (right for back, left for forward), never the Pencil and never a swipe that starts in a note.
- **A new line starts clean.** After a circled or highlighted line, Return no longer carries the circle or highlight onto the next line.

The same build is on TestFlight for iPhone and iPad (build 34).
