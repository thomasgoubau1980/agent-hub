title: Write your meeting notes during the meeting, in one note
---

A meeting's notes are now one yellow note, the same everywhere you see that meeting.

- **Meeting mode is a notepad beside the call.** It is the meeting's own note: write freely with bold and lists, and a line that starts with "+ " becomes a task in Akiflow when you press Return. The meeting's tasks sit under it, and the full page is one tap away.
- **The open meeting is tidier.** Its header shows who is in it. "Notes, live" while it is on, with the format buttons in the note's top row, then "Tasks, in Akiflow" under the note. Meeting mode is at the bottom while the meeting is on, and "Transcript in Fathom" once it has ended.
- **Write a meeting's notes on the day's note too.** Each meeting that has started is a section you can write in, marked "live" while it is on. It is the same note the meeting shows.
- **The day's note** now has its task field before the voice buttons, real bullets for "- " lines, and titles like "Note for tomorrow" and "Note for Mon 9 Nov".

The same build is on TestFlight for iPhone and iPad (build 30).
