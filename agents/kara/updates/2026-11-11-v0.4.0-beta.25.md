title: One Day page for every day
---

Today, Days, Triage, Review, Timer and the paper are now one page: the Day page. It looks the same for today and for any day you go back to.

- **The day at a glance.** Today's 3 at the top, then your day as a compact list, a detailed list or a calendar. Akiflow blocks carry their timers.
- **Meetings open in place.** Tap a meeting to see its notes, decisions and tasks without leaving the day.
- **The day's note.** Write on the day itself, open it as a full page, and add attachments.
- **The day in short.** Kara gathers the decisions and new tasks from your meetings and notes.
- **Find your way.** Tap the date for a month picker, or search every day Kara knows with the magnifier, ⌘K or /.
- **Recurring meetings.** Each one has its own page: every meeting, a decision log, and the tasks still open across the series.
- **Projects and the weekly review.** Your Notion projects are on the Day page, with a weekly review that walks through them one by one.

The same build is on TestFlight for iPhone and iPad (build 28).
