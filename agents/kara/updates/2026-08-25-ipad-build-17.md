version: iPad builds 17-18
date: 2026-08-25
title: The iPad becomes an all-day hub
---

Kara on iPad (via TestFlight) turns into a surface that can stay open all day. The day itself is the wide column, your personal layer sits in a fixed rail, and one button drops you into full focus.

- **The NOW hero** always answers one question: what is now? In a meeting it shows the meeting and what comes next; between meetings, the next thing with a countdown; on a clear day, your first open Top 3 task.
- **The day stream**: date header, hero, agenda, then the diary of finished meetings with their recording links and the tasks Kara caught, one tap each to approve, defer or reject.
- **The personal rail never moves**: goals of the week with tick-through (build 18 swapped these in for the duplicate Top 3, which already lives in the day column), your ruled note to self, and the Pencil sketch.
- **Full Focus**: the Focus button starts a pomodoro on your top open task and takes over the screen. The ring, the one task, pause and "Done, tick it off" writing through to Todoist, with a quiet footer naming what is next.
- **Also in these builds**: the Day Surface diary on iPhone and iPad, and the cloud-sync guard that stops older builds from deleting diary data. If the diary looks empty right after updating, it fills in at the next Kara sweep.
