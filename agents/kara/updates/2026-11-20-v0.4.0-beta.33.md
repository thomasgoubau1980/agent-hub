title: Notes of their own, in one Notes place
---

- **Notes, one place for every note.** Open it from the Kara menu, under Projects, or with ⌘⇧N on the Mac. Your own notes come first, pinned ones on top, then the most recently changed. The day and meeting notes follow, newest first.
- **Notes that belong to no day.** Tap New note: a blank page opens with the cursor on the first line, and that line becomes the note's name. It has the same fonts, page, format bar and Pencil as the day's note. A note closed without a word in it is not kept.
- **One search for all notes.** Type in the search box at the top of Notes. It finds your words in every note, ignoring capitals and accents, and shows the line they are in.
- **Pin, link, delete.** Pin a note to keep it on top, link it to a project so it shows on the project's page too, or delete it (Undo for a few seconds). Pages made earlier with "+ Page" are now notes of their own. A "+ " line in one reaches Akiflow with "From the note:" and its name.
- **A new task in Today's 3 reaches Akiflow again.** Akiflow refused tasks typed straight into Today's 3, and Kara showed "Akiflow did not answer". They now arrive in Akiflow, planned for that day.
- **The keyboard comes up where you tap.** A tap anywhere on a note's paper, in the margin or below the last line, puts the cursor there. On the iPhone and iPad, the full-page note has a keyboard button to bring the keyboard up or put it away.

The same build is on TestFlight for iPhone and iPad (build 36).
