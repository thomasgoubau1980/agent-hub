title: Delete a note you made, with Undo
---

A note you created can now be deleted. Before, a page could only be closed, and it stayed in Notes.

- **Where.** On a project's page, each note has "Delete" next to "Open". In the paper, an open page has "Delete" in its top bar.
- **Undo.** The note goes at once, and "Undo" shows for 8 seconds to bring it back exactly as it was.
- **Notion follows.** A few seconds later, the note's copy in your Notion Notes database goes to Notion's trash, where you can still restore it for 30 days.
- **What stays.** Next actions and tasks already sent from the note's "+ " lines are kept.

Only notes you made can be deleted: project notes and pages. A meeting's note and the day note belong to the meeting or the day; clear their text instead.

The Undo bar also shows on top of the paper now; before, the paper could hide it.
