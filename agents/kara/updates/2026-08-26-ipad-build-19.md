title: Release notes live inside the iOS app too
---

Same day as the desktop version: updates now have a home inside Kara on iPhone and iPad.

- **Profile gets an Updates card**: your version and build, the latest release's title, and a green dot when something new landed since you last looked.
- **iPhone**: the Updates screen is a feed of cards with a Desktop / iPad & iPhone filter; the newest entry opens expanded and any card expands in place.
- **iPad**: the same screen becomes master-detail: releases on the left, the selected entry's full notes on the right.
- Works offline from the last fetched feed, and everything reads the same published notes as [the public updates page](https://thomasgoubau1980.github.io/kara-updates/).
