title: Past days look like today, and a meeting opens as a note
---

- **Past days look like today.** Go back to any day and you get the same Day page, with Compact, Detailed and Calendar. Its meetings open in place with their recaps, Today's 3 shows how each ended, and below the day you see what was done and what was planned but not done.
- **A meeting opens as a note.** Tap a meeting and its notes are a yellow note, the same as the day's: bold and lists, a voice note, say a task, attachments, and its tasks for Akiflow. Notes you already wrote on it carry over.
- **Kara tells you when it is signed out of the Kara cloud.** Without it, meeting recaps from Fathom and Granola and notes from your other devices don't arrive. A red line on the Day page now says so, with Sign in.
- **No more Behind list at the top.** Late tasks belong in your Akiflow sweep, not in Kara.

The same build is on TestFlight for iPhone and iPad (build 29).
