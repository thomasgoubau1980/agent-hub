title: A shorter menu, and Notes one tap from today
---

- **Five places in the menu.** The Kara menu now lists Search, Projects, Notes, Triage and Settings, nothing else. Write and Today left the menu because the Day page already does both: ⌘J still opens the day's note, and "‹ Today" or T still brings you back. Recurring meetings are in Search, as before.
- **One line for your connections.** Instead of four status rows, the bottom of the menu says "All connected", or names the service that needs you, in red when it is an error. Tap it to open Settings, where each service shows its own status. On the Mac, hover the line to see all four.
- **The Kara logo is a button, and now looks like one.** A small badge in its corner shows that it opens the menu. The green dot for new updates keeps the other corner.
- **All notes, from today's note.** Today's yellow note has an "All notes" button in its header that opens Notes. On the iPhone it closes the note first.

The same build is on TestFlight for iPhone and iPad (build 37).
