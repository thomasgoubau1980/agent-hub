title: Connecting Notion works again, and Notion shows in the sidebar
---

Connecting Notion could stop with "OAuth state mismatch". It happened when you approved Kara in a Notion tab from an earlier try. Kara now accepts the approval from any Notion tab you opened since it started, so it no longer matters which one you use.

- **Never stuck waiting.** While Kara waits for Notion, "Open Notion again" opens a fresh tab, on Projects and in Settings.
- **Notion in the connections list.** The bottom of the sidebar now shows Notion next to Akiflow, Todoist and Google Calendar: off, the time your projects were last read, or "error" with the reason when you hover.
