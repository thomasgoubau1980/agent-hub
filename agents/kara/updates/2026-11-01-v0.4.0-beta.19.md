title: Kara tells you when Notion is signed in to the wrong workspace
---

If Notion is connected but your projects don't appear, Kara now tells you why instead of saying "Notion isn't answering".

- **Wrong workspace.** When Kara is signed in to a Notion workspace that doesn't hold your projects, it names that workspace and offers **Connect again**, on Projects and in Settings. On Notion's page, pick your private workspace at the top before approving.
- **The real reason.** When Notion truly fails, the banner now also shows what Notion said.
- **A reminder when connecting.** The connect screen asks you to pick the workspace that holds your projects.
