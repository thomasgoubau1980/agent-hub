title: Apple's own Pencil tools, and the Kara icon on iPhone and iPad
---

- **Apple's Pencil tools.** On the iPhone and iPad, open a note full page and tap the Pencil: Apple's own canvas and tool palette come up, the same as in Notes. Pens, fineliner, marker, pencil, eraser, lasso, ruler, colours, undo, and your palm does not draw. Tap the blue tick when you are done.
- **Drawings are kept safe.** A drawing is saved on the device straight away and sent to your Kara cloud; offline or signed out, it waits on the device, shows on the page, and goes up once you are back.
- **Drawings on the Mac.** The Mac shows every drawing where it was drawn. You edit drawings on the iPhone or iPad. Drawings made with Kara's earlier tools still show, and become Apple drawings the first time you open them on the iPad.
- **The right icon.** The iPhone and iPad app now has the Kara icon. Until now it showed a placeholder logo.

The same build is on TestFlight for iPhone and iPad (build 33).
