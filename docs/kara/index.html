Kara · User guide

Kara

Deep Focus productivity. One place for today's priorities, agenda, inbox triage and weekly review, on Mac, iPhone and iPad, with a Telegram agent doing the legwork.

livelatest: v0.4.0-beta.34release notes →

What Kara is

Kara is your day companion on the Mac, iPhone and iPad. It is one page: your day. It shows your priorities, your meetings and Akiflow slots, one note for the day, and at the bottom what was decided and what came out of it. Kara doesn't replace your other tools. Each tool keeps one job:

  • Akiflow holds your plan and all of your own tasks.
  • Notion (your private workspace) holds your projects, their next actions and your notes.
  • Google Calendar holds your time.
  • Todoist holds your team's actions and the suggestions Kara finds for you.
  • Kara is the one page you live in between meetings: today's plan, your meetings and what they produce, your notes and your projects.

Behind the app, the Kara agent reads your meetings and mail three times a day (09:30, 13:30 and 17:30) and suggests tasks from them.

Getting set up

Open Settings and connect, once per device:

  1. Akiflow. Your day then shows your Akiflow blocks with their tasks, and everything you write as a task lands in your Akiflow Inbox.
  2. Notion. Sign in with the account that holds your projects. Projects, project pages and notes then work.
  3. Google Calendar for your meetings, and Todoist for the team's actions and Kara's suggestions.

The bottom of the Kara menu shows one line for all four: "All connected" with a green dot when everything works, or the name of the service that needs you, in red. Tap it to open Settings, where each service shows its own status, the time it last read and any error.

Finding your way

Kara opens on today. The Kara logo at the top left, the one with the small badge, opens the menu. It lists the places you can go:

  • Search (⌘K): meetings, decisions and tasks, recurring meetings included.
  • Projects: your Notion projects and their next actions.
  • Notes (⌘⇧N on the Mac): every note you have written.
  • Triage: Kara's suggestions that are not tied to a meeting.
  • Settings: your connections, the release notes and this guide.

Every other screen has a ‹ Today button at the top to bring you back. Today's note also has an All notes button that opens Notes.

Your day page

From top to bottom:

  1. The date. Tap it for a month calendar; days with meetings have a dot. The magnifier next to it, or ⌘K, searches every meeting, decision and task. The arrows, a swipe, or ← and → move one day; T brings you back to today.
  2. Today's 3. Tap Pick today's 3 to choose them from your Akiflow week, or type a new one in the last line: Kara creates it in Akiflow, planned today, and tags it "today".
  3. Your day. Your meetings and Akiflow slots on one timeline, with a red line for now. Choose Compact, Detailed or Calendar above it. Show decides what else appears: declined and skipped meetings, done tasks, and what you hid.
  4. Projects, folded, with the weekly project review (see Projects).
  5. The day in short. Every decision with its meeting, every new task with a tick circle, and in the evening what is still open, with a Tomorrow button to move it.

Akiflow slots and the timer

A focus or admin slot shows the tasks Akiflow planned in it. Tick them off on the page; they are done in Akiflow too. A slot with open tasks has Start 25 min; when more than 25 minutes are left, Rest of the slot runs the timer to its end. One timer runs at a time.

Other days

A past day looks like today, read back: its meetings with their recaps, its slots, its note and its day in short with what was left open. You can add to a past day's note or a past meeting's note and tick off what is still open; Kara never moves or deletes anything there. Go forward from today for tomorrow: its calendar and a Note for tomorrow.

Meetings

Tap a meeting to open it: the recap, the decisions, the detailed outline from Fathom or Granola, your notes and its tasks, which go to Akiflow. Before and during the meeting your notes are there, and the recap follows when it ends. Until Kara has read the meeting, it says "Not read yet".

Meeting mode (on the live meeting, or ⌃⌥M from any app on the Mac) is the meeting's note in a narrow window beside the call, kept on top, with the same fonts, page and format bar.

Every meeting has a + Project chip. Put a meeting on a project once, and Kara asks whether the next meetings in the series keep it. Its notes then show on the project's page, and its "+ " lines become that project's next actions.

Recurring meetings

A recurring meeting has a small ↻ on the timeline. In the meeting, the "see every …" link opens its page; Search finds it too. The page lists every time it happened, newest first, with recap, decisions, tasks and notes. Decision log shows only the decisions; Open tasks shows what is still open across the series. Before the next one, it lists what is still open and gives you a Notes to prepare post-it.

Writing: your notes

There is one note per day. Press ⌘J on any screen, or on the iPhone tap the yellow Note button. On the unfolded iPhone Duo, the iPad and the Mac it also sits on the right of the day. A meeting's note and its part of the day's note are the same note: write in either. Voice note adds what you say, written out.

The line rules

Every note in Kara works the same way, and the same line rules apply everywhere:

  • A plain line is a note.
  • A line starting with "+ " is a task. On the day's note it goes to your Akiflow Inbox; on a project's note, or a meeting that belongs to a project, it becomes that project's next action.
  • "@name" is something you wait on from someone.
  • "! " is a decision.
  • A checklist item (the checklist button) stays in the note. It is not a task.

A line is handed on once, when you press Return at its end or close the note, so nothing is sent twice. Every note is also saved to the Notes database in your Notion a few seconds after you stop typing.

Make it yours

Tap Aa in the note's top row:

  • Font: Patrick Hand, Kalam, Caveat, Dancing Script, Kaushan Script, Amatic SC, Homemade Apple or the system font. Small, medium or large.
  • Page: plain, lines, squares or dots.
  • Use for all notes: the font and page become the default for every note without a choice of its own.

A note is yellow, on lines, in Patrick Hand until you change it.

Formatting

On the iPhone and iPad, a bar sits above the keyboard while you write: checklist, bullets, Title, underline, strike through, highlight, circle, Bold and Italic. On the Mac, select some text and the same bar appears above it; ⌘B, ⌘I and ⌘U work too. A tap anywhere on the paper brings up the keyboard.

Writing with the Apple Pencil

Open a note full page (the expand icon in its top row). Write with the Pencil anywhere in the text and Scribble turns it into type. To draw, tap the Pencil button: Apple's tool palette appears, the same as in Notes. Your palm does not draw. Tap the tick to stop drawing. The Mac shows drawings as pictures; you edit them on the iPhone or iPad.

Photos and documents

The paperclip on the full page adds a photo (camera or library), a document or today's date. They are kept in your Kara cloud.

Projects

Projects in the Kara menu shows every active project from your Notion as a card with its next actions.

  • Add a next action by typing under a project and pressing Enter. It is saved in Notion with the project's code (for example "[BRD] Send the pre-read"), reaches your Akiflow Inbox within a few minutes, and Kara files it under the matching Akiflow project. A small "to Akiflow" dot shows until it has arrived.
  • Tick it off here or in Akiflow: it is done in both.
  • Later, Done or Drop from the ••• on a project. Later projects fold at the bottom.
  • + New project needs only a name. Kara makes the code and creates it in Notion. If Akiflow has no project with that name yet, Kara tells you once, with a "Copy name" button.

A project's page

Click a project's name to open its page:

  • Description: a few lines on what the project is and why it matters. Write it right there; it is saved in the project's Objective field in Notion.
  • Done when: what finished looks like, in one sentence.
  • Next actions and Waiting for (the "@name" lines in the project's notes).
  • Notes, newest first, including the notes of its meetings. "+ New note" starts one.
  • Linked, on the right: decks, documents and chats that belong to the project in Notion. Paste a link to add one; hide the panel in one click.

Project codes

Every project has a short code of three or four capitals, and every action's title starts with it. Akiflow brings actions over from Notion without their project, so the code is how Kara keeps Notion and Akiflow in step. Keep the code when you edit a title.

The weekly project review

On the day page, the folded Projects section starts with one line: Weekly project review, with the number of projects and the due day (Friday by default; you can run it any day). Tap it to open the review. On the iPhone it slides in from the right; on the unfolded Duo, iPad and Mac it fills the screen, with your projects on the left.

Kara shows one project at a time: Done when, the Next step (edit it in place), the Open actions (tick them off) and Last week's meetings. Type a new action and press Return: it becomes a next action in Notion and lands in Akiflow a few minutes later. Next project moves on, Skip comes back to it at the end, Close this project closes it in Notion. When you finish, the line on the day says the review is done.

Notes

Notes in the Kara menu, under Projects, keeps every note you have written in one place. On the Mac, ⌘⇧N opens it. You can also open it from today's note: tap All notes in its header.

  • Your notes come first: notes that belong to no day or meeting. Pinned ones are on top, then the most recently changed.
  • Day and meeting notes follow, newest first. They still live on their day and meeting; Notes lets you find them and opens them full page.
  • Search, at the top, looks through every note, names and text, ignoring capitals and accents. Each result shows the line that matches.

Tap New note to start one. A blank page opens with the cursor on the first line, and that line becomes the note's name. Everything from the day's note works here: Aa for the font and page, the format bar, the Apple Pencil, and the line rules ("+ " a task, "@name" a waiting-for, "! " a decision). A "+ " line reaches your Akiflow Inbox with "From the note:" and the note's name. A note you close without writing in it is not kept.

Under each of your notes:

  • Pin keeps it at the top.
  • Link to a project puts it on that project's page too, and its "+ " lines become the project's next actions.
  • Delete removes it, with Undo for a few seconds; its Notion copy goes to Notion's trash.
  • Only on this device shows while a note has not reached the Kara cloud (signed out, or offline). It goes once the note is synced.

Triage

Triage is the one queue of what Kara suggests from your meetings, mail and Slack. Three choices for each: Keep sends it to your Akiflow Inbox, Reference files it, Reject drops it (with Undo). Kara never asks when; that is planning, and planning happens in Akiflow.

On the iPhone Duo, iPad and Mac

Folded, Kara is one column and a meeting opens in place. Unfolded, and on the iPad and the Mac, the day stays on the left and what you open sits on the right; with nothing open, the right side shows the day's note.

Good to know

  • Nothing fails silently. When tasks stop reaching Akiflow, or the agent stops running, the day shows a red line saying so.
  • Updates. Settings › Updates lists every release with what changed. Kara offers a new version when it starts and every 6 hours; "Update & restart" installs it.
  • Usage. Kara counts which features you use (names and counts only, never what you write). One switch in Settings turns it off.
  • Two Macs. Your notes and settings sync between Macs. Which project a meeting belongs to is kept on each Mac for now.
  • Offline. Your notes and ticks are kept and sent once you are back online.
  • Older versions. A note opened on an older Kara shows its formatting marks as plain characters (for example ==8%==). Nothing is lost; the new Kara shows them as formatting again.
